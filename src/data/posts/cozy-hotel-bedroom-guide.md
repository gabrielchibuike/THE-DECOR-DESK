We spend a third of our lives in our bedrooms, yet they are often the last spaces we style. We prioritize public spaces like the living room or kitchen, leaving the master suite as a repository for laundry piles and mismatched furniture.

If you've ever checked into a boutique hotel and instantly felt a wave of calm wash over you, you know how powerful a well-designed bedroom can be. Here is how to recreate that five-star hotel luxury right in your own home.

## 1. Upgrade to Premium Linen Bedding
The cornerstone of any luxury hotel bed is the quality of the bedding. Ditch synthetic microfiber sheets and invest in a pure French flax linen duvet set. Linen regulates body temperature, has a beautiful natural drape, and possesses a textured, relaxed look that only gets better with age.

## 2. Master the Pillow Stack
A flat bed looks uninviting. Create a plush, layered look by combining different pillow shapes. Start with two large Euro shams propped up against the headboard, followed by your standard sleeping pillows, and finish with a long lumbar cushion in a contrasting fabric like heavy linen or boucle.

## 3. Design Layered Ambient Lighting
Never rely on a single harsh overhead light. Hotels excel at lighting because they layer sources. Include dimmable warm table lamps on each bedside table, install wall-mounted reading sconces, and add floor uplighting to cast soft glows across the walls at night.

## 4. Clear the Clutter from Bedside Tables
Keep the bedside environment clear of unnecessary items. Make sure your nightstands have drawers to hide charging cables and books. On top, keep only the essentials: a small dish for jewelry, a ceramic carafe for water, and a single bud vase.
